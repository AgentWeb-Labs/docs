AgentWeb supports two distinct payment concepts. Understanding the difference helps you configure the right level of payment authority for your agent.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Credits" icon="coins">
    Credits pay for execution — approximately one credit per completed action. Credits are included in your plan and are auto-refunded if an action fails before completion.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AgentWeb Card" icon="credit-card">
    The AgentWeb Card lets your agent pay for purchases on external websites using one-time virtual cards. Available on **Pro and Max** plans only.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  On the Free plan, your agent cannot make purchases on external websites. Upgrade to Pro or Max to enable the AgentWeb Card and agent-initiated checkout.
</Note>

## Set up the AgentWeb Card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to your dashboard">
    Go to your AgentWeb dashboard and navigate to the payment settings section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enroll your payment card">
    Enroll your real payment card using a device-bound passkey. Your real card number is stored in the secure vault and is never shown to your agent, surfaced in conversation, or transmitted to merchants during purchases.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set spending limits">
    Configure a per-purchase spending limit and a daily limit. Your agent cannot approve or initiate a purchase that exceeds either limit, and the limits can be updated from the dashboard at any time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agent is ready to purchase">
    Once enrollment is complete, your agent can mint a one-time virtual card for each transaction. The virtual card is charged to your enrolled payment method, but merchants never see your real card number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How agent purchases work

Before your agent initiates any purchase, it calls `agentweb_price_setup` to retrieve the current package details, usage meters, and payment-authority requirements for the workflow. This call gives your agent the full picture of what a purchase will involve before asking for your approval.

When a paid action is ready to proceed, your agent summarizes exactly what will be purchased — including the item, amount, recipient site, and any relevant limits — and waits for your explicit approval. Your agent does not proceed without it.

After you approve, your agent calls `agentweb_create_checkout_session` to obtain a Dodo hosted checkout handoff URL. You are redirected to the Dodo checkout flow in your browser, where the transaction is completed. AgentWeb grants entitlement — and any associated credits — only after a Dodo-signed webhook verifies the payment state. A checkout redirect alone is not proof of entitlement.

## Payment authority

Every agent-initiated payment requires a delegated payment authority object that defines exactly what the agent is permitted to do. Here is an example:

```json
{
  "authority_id": "auth_123",
  "principal": {
    "type": "business",
    "id": "example_co",
    "agent_provider": "claude_code"
  },
  "scope": ["price.setup", "create.checkout_session"],
  "amount_limit": { "value": 500000, "interval": "single_use" },
  "currency": "usd",
  "expires_at": "2026-05-08T00:00:00Z",
  "revocation_url": "https://example.com/agent-authority/auth_123/revoke",
  "receipt_required": true,
  "verification_required": true
}
```

Key fields at a glance:

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `authority_id` | Unique identifier for this authority grant |
| `principal` | Who the agent is acting on behalf of and which agent provider is involved |
| `scope` | The specific operations the agent is permitted to invoke |
| `amount_limit` | Maximum spend value and whether the authority is single-use or recurring |
| `currency` | Currency for the amount limit |
| `expires_at` | Hard expiry after which the authority is no longer valid |
| `revocation_url` | URL to call to immediately cancel the authority |
| `receipt_required` | Whether a receipt must be returned after the transaction |
| `verification_required` | Whether a signed verification artifact is required |

<Warning>
  Agents must never collect raw card numbers in conversation. Any request that asks your agent to handle, repeat, or forward card numbers should be refused.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  A checkout redirect is **not** proof of entitlement. Your agent must wait for Dodo webhook signature verification before treating a purchase as complete or granting any downstream access.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  You can revoke payment authority at any time from your AgentWeb dashboard without losing your execution history or any previously verified receipts.
</Tip>
