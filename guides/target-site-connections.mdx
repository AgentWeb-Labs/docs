title: "Connect Target-Site Accounts for Action Execution"
sidebarTitle: "Site Connections"

---

Some AgentWeb actions require access to your account on an external website — for example, your Amazon account to track an order, or your Hermes account to manage a booking. A target-site connection lets AgentWeb vault that credential securely and use it only within approved workflows. Your agent never sees the credential, and neither do the action's inputs.

`read_page` and `discover` never require a target-site connection. A connection is only needed for actions whose metadata indicates an authenticated external account is required.

## Connect a target-site account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check if the selected action requires a connection">
    Call `list_actions` on the target domain after authenticating with your AgentWeb key. Inspect the metadata for the action you want to run and look for `account_required: true`. If the field is absent or false, you can proceed without a connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    Ask your agent to call `agentweb_start_connection` for the target site. The tool returns a `connect_url` — a one-time URL you open in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete sign-in">
    Open the returned `connect_url` in your browser and sign in to the target site as you normally would. AgentWeb vaults the resulting session in your account — your credentials are never passed through the agent conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Poll for completion">
    While you complete sign-in, your agent polls `agentweb_poll_connection` until it reports `connected: true`. Once polling returns a final connected state, the session is ready for use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Proceed with execution">
    With the connection in place, your agent can call `execute` on the action. AgentWeb uses your vaulted target-site account within the approved workflow — no further manual intervention is needed for that session.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cookie-backed providers

Some providers, such as Hermes, use a cookie-backed connection model. For these providers, follow this sequence:

1. Your agent calls `agentweb_auth_status` or `agentweb_connection_status` to check whether the provider is already connected.
2. If not connected, your agent calls `agentweb_start_connection` and sends you to the returned `connect_url`.
3. Complete sign-in in your browser.
4. Your agent polls `agentweb_poll_connection` until it returns `connected: true`.

Once the poll resolves, proceed with your action as normal.

## Connection lifecycle

Understanding how connections behave over time helps you plan for long-running or repeated workflows:

- **Isolation** — Connections are customer-specific. Each user's vaulted session is isolated and never shared across accounts.
- **Inspection and revocation** — You can view active connections, check their status, or revoke them at any time from your AgentWeb dashboard.
- **Expiry during execution** — If a connection expires while an action is running, your agent receives a structured failure that points to the reconnection step. Re-run the connection flow and retry.
- **Hard stops** — Your agent must stop and surface the issue to you if a connection is expired, refused by the target site, or attached to the wrong account. It must not attempt to work around a failed connection state.

<Warning>
  A connected account does not grant blanket permission. AgentWeb still checks scopes, approval, and limits on every consequential action — a valid connection is a necessary condition, not a sufficient one.
</Warning>
