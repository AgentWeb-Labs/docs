AgentWeb uses a single credit balance across your entire account. Credits pay for execution — the work of completing a task on a real website. Only verified completions are billed, and failed actions are automatically refunded. You always see the estimated credit cost before a run starts.

## How credits work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What counts as one credit?">
    One credit equals one executed action — sending a message, submitting a form, or placing an order step. Larger jobs are simply more actions. For example, a job application is around 4 credits, and filing taxes is around 25. Reading pages is free.

    AgentWeb shows the exact credit cost before every run, so the estimates on this page never surprise you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do failed tasks cost credits?">
    No. If AgentWeb fails to complete a task, the credit is automatically refunded. You are only billed for work that verifiably completes. If you expand the scope mid-task, the added work gets a fresh estimate first.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can my agent pay for things?">
    On Pro and Max, yes. You enroll your card once with a device-bound passkey and set per-purchase and daily limits. Your agent then mints a one-time virtual card per purchase — merchants never see your real card, and every payment comes with a receipt and verification.

    On Free, your agent cannot make purchases.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do credits pay for purchases?">
    No. Credits pay for execution — the work of completing the task. Purchases are charged to your enrolled card, within the limits you set. The two never mix.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will I see the credit cost before a task starts?">
    Yes, always. AgentWeb shows the estimated credit cost before the run and only prompts you about your balance if the task would leave you low.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when I run out of credits?">
    If a task needs more credits than you have, you are prompted to top up before the run starts. Nothing runs, and nothing is charged, until you have enough balance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does annual billing work?">
    Annual plans are 30% off the monthly rate, billed once per year. All twelve months of credits are granted upfront and expire at the end of the annual billing period.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  100 credits ≈ 100 completed actions. Start with the Free plan to explore AgentWeb before upgrading.
</Note>
