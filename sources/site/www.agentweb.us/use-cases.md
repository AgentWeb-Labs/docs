## The ops lead

40-person company, six countries

Payroll runs across Gusto for employees and Deel for contractors, reconciled against the bank feed. Benefits and business insurance renewed before they lapse. She approves the numbers; her agent does the portal work.

- Run payroll across Gusto and Deel, then reconcile against the bank feed
- Renew our business insurance before it lapses

route 02

## The accountant

30 clients, four deadlines a quarter

Quarterly sales-tax filings in every registered state. 1099s for every contractor at year end. Receipts chased out of vendor portals and invoices collected with escalating reminders. Every filing comes back with a receipt.

- File quarterly sales tax in every state we're registered in
- Prepare and file 1099s for all contractors

route 03

## The travel desk

200 flights a month, one policy

Corporate flights booked within policy, fare holds while approvals clear. When the conference moves, the whole team is rebooked overnight, and visa appointments arrive with the document packets already compiled.

- Book 50 corporate flights this month within travel policy
- Rebook the team when the conference dates move
