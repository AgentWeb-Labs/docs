# Source: https://www.agentweb.us/privacy

## Introduction

Agent Web Labs, Inc. ("we," "us," or "our") operates agentweb.us and the AgentWeb platform (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect personal information and user content when you use the Service. It applies wherever we offer the Service, subject to applicable law.

## Notice at Collection

We collect identifiers and account information; action, prompt, request, response, and connection information; approval and receipt records; usage and device information; and information you choose to provide. Content sent through the Service may include voice transcripts, prompts, instructions, tool parameters, AI-generated responses, action results, and error details. We use this information to provide, secure, analyze, troubleshoot, develop, and improve the Service, maintain records, support users, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell or rent personal information or user content, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## Information We Collect

- **Account information:** email address, name if supplied through sign-in, and account-authentication data.
- **Action and content information:** prompts, instructions, tool names and parameters, URLs and destination domains, selected options, recipient details, voice transcripts, AI-generated responses, action results, execution status, receipts, and error information sent to or returned through AgentWeb. If your agent client includes a full transcript, response, or other content in an AgentWeb request or result, we may receive that content.
- **MCP and agent analytics:** MCP initialization, tool-list, tool-call, and exception events; request parameters and response data; tool intent, target domain and action; timestamps, duration, status, client, account tier, user or anonymous identifier, and related diagnostic information. Parameters and responses may be sanitized, masked, or truncated before analytics processing, but those measures may not remove every sensitive value.
- **Approval and authority records:** approval decisions, execution-mode selection, consent statement, scope, client, timestamp, revocation, and applicable spending or permission limits.
- **Connection information:** connection status and encrypted credentials, tokens, or session material needed to use an account you explicitly connect. We do not expose connected-account credentials to other users or to your agent client.
- **Payment information:** payment-approval and transaction metadata, including transaction amount, merchant, status, and applicable fees, needed to enforce limits and generate receipts. When you enroll a card for one-time agent purchases, Basis Theory collects and stores the card details directly. We receive a secure token reference, but do not collect or store raw payment-card numbers in AgentWeb systems.
- **Website and product analytics:** account or anonymous identifiers, email address, page URLs and paths, referrer, signup, login, connection, payment, API-key, referral, newsletter, and other product events, browser and device information, IP address, approximate region, and event timing. Depending on our PostHog project settings, session replay may also record page interactions and visible page content, including text or input content that is not successfully masked.
- **Waitlist, lead, newsletter, and support information:** email addresses and other information entered into signup, login, waitlist, newsletter, support, or feedback forms. We may record a valid email after it is entered in a signup or login form even if you do not complete or submit the registration.
- **Referral information:** referral codes, clicks, redemption and reward events, and identifiers associated with a referrer or referred account.

## Agent and Voice Content

AgentWeb runs inside the AI agent or interface you choose to use. We do not receive an entire conversation, chat history, voice recording, or file merely because AgentWeb is installed. We do receive content that your client sends to AgentWeb or that AgentWeb returns. This may include a full voice transcript, prompt, AI response, tool parameter, file content, or other sensitive information when it is included in a request, response, recorded browser session, log, or analytics event. You should provide only information necessary for the requested action and only information you are authorized to provide.

## How We Use Information

- Provide, operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Service.
- Analyze prompts, transcripts, requests, responses, results, usage patterns, and errors to evaluate and improve reliability, action maps, product features, user experience, and support.
- Authenticate users, manage accounts, and connect authorized third-party accounts.
- Execute a requested action in the execution mode and within the limits you select.
- Create action records, receipts, and consent or approval audit logs.
- Communicate about support, account security, product updates, and service changes.
- Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized activity, and policy violations.
- Comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes.

## How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent personal information or user content, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We disclose information as needed to provide and improve the Service, including to a third-party website or provider you direct us to use and to vendors supporting authentication, hosting, analytics, email, security, payment, and customer support. Current examples include Supabase for authentication and data storage, Cloudflare and Vercel for infrastructure and traffic data, PostHog for product, website, MCP, error, and session analytics, Resend and Beehiiv for email, and Basis Theory and our payment providers for payment services. Data sent to PostHog may include the analytics, identifiers, request content, response content, voice transcripts, AI output, and session information described above. We do not authorize our service providers to sell this data or use it for their own advertising. Providers may process information under our applicable agreements and their own terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, in connection with a corporate transaction, or with your direction or consent.

## Data Retention

We retain personal information and user content for as long as reasonably necessary to provide, analyze, secure, and improve the Service, maintain account and action records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and enforce our agreements. Retention periods vary by data type, purpose, account status, provider configuration, backup cycle, and legal requirement. Analytics data, including content sent to PostHog, may be retained according to our configured PostHog retention period and backup practices. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information by contacting [support@agentweb.us](mailto:support@agentweb.us). We may retain limited information where required or permitted by law.

## Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, including encryption in transit and at rest where supported by the relevant system. No system is completely secure, and you should protect your account credentials, API keys, and connected accounts.

## Cookies and Analytics

We use essential cookies and similar technologies for authentication, session management, security, preferences, and referral attribution. We also use Vercel Analytics and PostHog. PostHog analytics may begin when the Service loads or receives a request and may associate activity with an anonymous identifier, account identifier, email address, or hashed network identifier. PostHog stores its analytics identifier in browser local storage so the same device can be recognized across page loads and OAuth redirects. This storage is cookie-free but persists until PostHog resets it or browser storage is cleared. PostHog may process page URLs, product events, session replay data, and MCP request and response analytics as described above. We do not use this data for third-party advertising. Where applicable law gives you a right to object to, restrict, or withdraw consent for analytics processing, contact [support@agentweb.us](mailto:support@agentweb.us). A privacy notice does not replace consent where applicable law requires consent.
