## How credits work

01How does AgentWeb pricing work?

One account, one credit balance. Your agent spends credits to complete tasks on real websites - sending, submitting, booking, buying - and every task returns a verified completion receipt. Credits work across every supported site, so LinkedIn outreach, tax filing, order tracking, and checkout all draw from the same balance.

02What counts as one credit?

One credit is one executed action - sending a message, submitting a form, placing an order step. Bigger jobs are just more actions: a job application is around 4 credits, filing your taxes around 25. Reading pages is free. AgentWeb shows the exact credit cost before every run, so the estimates on this page never surprise you.

03Can my agent pay for things?

On Pro and Max, yes. You enroll your card once with a device-bound passkey and set per-purchase and daily limits. Your agent can then pay for services, orders, and subscriptions directly from your connector - Claude Code, Codex, or any MCP tool - using a one-time card minted per purchase, so merchants never see your real card. Every payment comes with a receipt and verification. On Free, your agent cannot buy anything.

04Do credits pay for purchases?

No. Credits pay for execution - the work of completing the task. Purchases are charged to your own enrolled card, within the limits you set. The two never mix.

05Will I see the credit cost before a task starts?

Yes. AgentWeb shows the estimated credit cost before the run, and only nudges you about balance if the task would leave you low.

06Do failed tasks cost credits?

No. If AgentWeb fails to complete a task, the retry is on us. You are only billed for work that verifiably completes. If you expand the scope mid-task, the added work gets a fresh estimate first.

07How does annual billing work?

Annual plans are 30% off the monthly rate - Pro drops from $50 to $35 a month, Max from $200 to $140 - billed once per year. All twelve months of credits are granted upfront and expire at the end of the annual billing period.

08What happens when I run out of credits?

If a task needs more credits than you have, you are prompted to top up before the run starts. Nothing runs, and nothing is charged, without enough balance.

09How is checkout protected?

Checkout is created server-side from approved plan choices, validated credit quantities, the authenticated customer, and payment eligibility - the pricing page itself never handles payment identifiers.
