\# AgentWeb Pricing AgentWeb gives each customer one credit balance for internet execution. Credits work across every supported website, app, and account. Credits pay for AgentWeb work. External purchases, subscriptions, invoices, and merchant payments use a separately funded AgentWeb Card on Pro and Max. ## Free $0/month Includes 100 AgentWeb credits. - Universal credits across websites and accounts - Research, drafts, forms, and account prep - Transparent credit estimate before each run - No card payments or external purchases - Low-balance prompt only when credits are tight ## Pro $50/month Includes 2,500 AgentWeb credits per month. - Everything in Free - AgentWeb Card available with funded balance - Payments require approval and receipts - Priority execution - Top-up credits and monthly extra usage - 2 concurrent task runs ## Max $200/month Includes 12,000 AgentWeb credits per month. - Everything in Pro - Higher card limits with approval controls - Highest execution priority - More concurrent task runs - Volume extra usage pricing - Priority support ## Enterprise Custom For custom volume, compliance, procurement, private workflows, and SLA support. - Everything in Max - Custom credit, concurrency, and card limits - Security review and procurement support - Dedicated onboarding through support@agentweb.us ## Top-up credits Purchased credits add extra capacity when a task would take the account below the required balance. | Pack | Price | Credits | Rate | | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | | Starter top-up | $10 | 400 | 2.5c / credit | | Growth top-up | $25 | 1,250 | 2.0c / credit | | Power top-up | $75 | 5,000 | 1.5c / credit | ## Find the right plan The pricing page includes an extra-credit calculator. Customers can select Pro or Max, move the monthly extra-credit slider from 0 to 435,000 credits, and see the included credits, total monthly credits, extra usage cost, effective extra rate, and estimated monthly total. ## Monthly extra usage For customers that want a variable monthly credit line from 0 to 435,000 extra credits. This equals up to $5,000 in self-serve extra usage before payment-processing fees. Larger monthly usage should go through support@agentweb.us. | Monthly extra credits | Rate | | --- | ---: | | 0 to 50,000 | $0.015 / credit | | 50,001 to 250,000 | $0.012 / credit | | 250,001 to 435,000 | $0.010 / credit | Example: 435,000 extra credits in one month equals $5,000 in extra usage revenue before payment-processing fees. Anything above that should go through support@agentweb.us. ## Credit calculation A credit is one verified action. Executing a mapped action reserves 1 credit, and the charge settles only when the action completes; every charge returns a receipt with a support ID. If an action fails, the reserved credit is automatically refunded to the balance. Discovery and read calls are not metered: discover, list\_actions, read\_page, and usage checks are free and never debit credits. AgentWeb does not keep pushing the remaining balance unless the task would leave the account low or the account does not have enough credits to start. Retries caused by AgentWeb do not count. If the user changes the scope, asks for more records, or approves a new branch of work, the added actions are billable at the same rate. ## Billing guardrails Customer-facing pages do not expose Dodo product IDs, add-on IDs, prices, quantities, currencies, discounts, tax values, or account IDs.
