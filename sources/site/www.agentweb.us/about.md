We were born in the same hospital, eight days apart. Then somehow we just kept ending up in the same places. Same kindergarten, same schools, same long afternoons messing with computers, breaking things, fixing them, and pretending we knew more than we did. Around 14, Andrey got Egor into writing software. It was not some big master plan. It was just fun, then it got serious, then it became the thing we kept coming back to. AgentWeb came from a very simple frustration. Agents look magical until you ask them to do something real on the internet. Log in. Book something. Buy something. Fill out a form. Use a messy website. Know when to ask for approval. Prove what they did. That part is still broken. So we are building AgentWeb to help agents actually finish tasks on the internet :) We do not have everything figured out. We are two people, building fast, learning fast, and trying to be honest about what works and what does not. But this is the thing we want to work on for a long time. If you try it, break it, hate it, love it, or use it for something weird, please tell us. We read everything.

With love, 
Andrey and Egor
